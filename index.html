About the Game

What is EA Sports FC 26?

EA Sports FC 26 is the latest instalment in EA's flagship football franchise, launching globally in autumn 2025. Building on the foundation of FC 25, the game delivers deeper gameplay mechanics, enhanced HyperMotionV technology, and an overhauled Ultimate Team experience.

🎮

HyperMotionV

Volumetric motion-capture data from hundreds of real matches powers the most authentic player movement and ball physics in the series.

🧠

FC IQ

Intelligent tactical AI adapts to your team's real-world formations and playing styles so every club behaves true to life.

⚡

Rush Mode

The fan-favourite 5v5 Rush mode returns with new arenas, ranked playlists, and seasonal rewards for competitive players.

🌍

Women's Football

Expanded women's club and international content with more leagues, authentic kits, and dedicated career pathways.
